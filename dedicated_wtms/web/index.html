Daily water usage

Usage is measured from the level: while the pump is idle, usage is the drop in volume; while it runs, the recent idle usage rate is assumed to continue, so the pump intake rate and daily totals are estimates (the sensor resolves about 1 L). Rates are averaged over a few samples to hide the sensor's 1 L steps.
