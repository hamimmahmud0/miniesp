# Maintainers

Lead: Hamim Mahmud ([@hamimmahmud0](https://github.com/hamimmahmud0)), a BUET student.

## We are looking for co-maintainers
There is no budget. This is for learning, CV credit and a fun hard problem (an operating system in about 283 KB of RAM).
A co-maintainer would:
- triage issues and review pull requests (about 2-3 hours a week)
- cut releases and keep the package journal healthy
- own an area: shell, networking/SSH, packages, drivers, or porting to ESP32-S3/C3

Interested? Open a Discussion titled "Maintainer interest" and say which area you would take.
Regular contributors who land three reviewed pull requests get commit access.
